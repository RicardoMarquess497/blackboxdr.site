Who we are

A small studio with a simple idea.

Learning should feel clear from the inside out. No hidden steps, no hidden fees, no black box.

Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: October 2026

30-day refund policy

If you are not satisfied with a purchase, email us within 30 days of the charge and we will issue a full refund to your original payment method. No complicated forms.

Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time from your account area or by emailing support. Cancellation stops all future charges, and you keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Processing time

Approved refunds are processed within five business days. Depending on your bank, the amount may take five to ten business days to appear on your statement.

FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Can't find your answer? Our support team is one email away.

How do I access my product after purchase?

Right after checkout you receive an email with your login details and a link to the app or members area. Access is instant and works on phone, tablet and desktop.

Is this a one-time payment or a subscription?

It depends on the product. Each checkout page clearly states whether the price is a one-time payment or a recurring subscription, along with the billing frequency and amount.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Send an email to our support address with the email used at purchase. We will confirm the cancellation or process your refund according to our 30-day refund policy.

Will the charge show up as "Black Box"?

Yes. Charges appear on your statement under the Black Box name, so you can always recognize your purchase.
